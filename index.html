Conceptual Framework & Paper

Beyond the Dashboard: A Conceptual Framework for Evaluating Insight Quality in AI-Augmented Business Intelligence Systems

Author: Md Masud Parvej (Independent Researcher)

Addresses a critical gap in augmented analytics: while modern BI systems automate pattern detection and natural language narration, organizations lack systematic standards to evaluate if AI-generated insights are statistically sound, non-trivial, and actionable. Proposes the Insight Quality Evaluation (IQE) Framework, synthesizing data mining interestingness, decision support systems, and information systems success research.

The 4 Evaluative Dimensions of the IQE Framework:

1. Statistical Validity

Ensures discovered patterns are genuine and non-spurious, guarding against overfitting and multiple-comparison noise.

2. Contextual Relevance

Evaluates whether an automated observation bears directly on active managerial decision scenarios.

3. Novelty

Measures added informational value beyond baseline organizational knowledge and known seasonal effects.

4. Actionability

Assesses whether an insight implies a clear, feasible, and high-impact business intervention.
